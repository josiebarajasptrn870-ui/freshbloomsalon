Fresh Bloom Salon Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

Fresh Bloom Salon respects your privacy and handles personal information in accordance with applicable UK data protection law, including the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices and rights available to you.

  1. Information we may collect

Depending on how you interact with us, we may collect your name, contact details, appointment and booking information, service preferences, and communications with us. We may also collect relevant information you choose to provide about allergies, sensitivities, or other health-related considerations so we can provide services safely. If you make a payment, payment details are generally processed by our payment provider; we may receive information about the payment, such as its status.

If you use our website, we may collect technical information such as your IP address, browser type, and information about how you use the site. Cookies or similar technologies may also be used where applicable. You can manage cookies through your browser settings and, where provided, our cookie controls.

  1. How we use your information

We may use personal information to manage appointments, provide salon services, respond to enquiries, process payments, maintain appropriate business records, and communicate important information about your bookings or our services. With your consent where required, we may also send you marketing messages. You can opt out of marketing at any time by using the unsubscribe option or contacting us.

We use health-related information only where necessary to consider your safety and provide or adapt a service. We will seek your explicit consent where required by law.

  1. Our lawful bases

We process personal information where necessary to perform a contract with you or take steps at your request before entering into a contract; to meet legal obligations; for our legitimate interests, such as managing and improving our business, provided those interests do not override your rights; or with your consent. Where we process special-category information, such as health information, we will also rely on an applicable additional condition under data protection law.

  1. Sharing your information

We may share information with service providers who help us run our business, such as booking, payment, website, email, or IT providers. They may use the information only to provide services to us and must protect it appropriately. We may also disclose information where required by law or to establish, exercise, or defend legal claims. We do not sell your personal information.

  1. International transfers

If a service provider processes personal information outside the United Kingdom, we will take steps required by law to ensure an appropriate level of protection, such as using an adequacy decision or approved contractual safeguards.

  1. How long we keep information

We keep personal information only for as long as needed for the purposes described in this policy, including providing services, maintaining business and financial records, resolving disputes, and meeting legal obligations. Retention periods depend on the type of information and why we hold it. When information is no longer needed, we will securely delete or anonymise it.

  1. Your rights

Depending on the circumstances, you may have the right to request access to your personal information, have it corrected or deleted, restrict or object to its processing, request data portability, or withdraw consent. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing carried out before withdrawal. You also have the right to complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk. We would appreciate the opportunity to address your concerns first.

  1. Security

We use appropriate technical and organisational measures to help protect personal information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to prevent unauthorised access, loss, or misuse.

  1. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will be made available through our usual customer information channels, with the updated date shown above.

  1. Contact us

For questions about this policy or to exercise your data protection rights, contact Fresh Bloom Salon using the contact details provided on our website or in your booking confirmation.
